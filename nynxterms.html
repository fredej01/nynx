Part Two — Privacy Policy

11 Information We Collect

When you use Nynx, we collect the following information solely for the purpose of transmitting the secure link:

  • Sender initials (up to 4 characters) — used for display purposes only, not stored long-term;
  • Recipient email address — used to deliver the secure link;
  • The SSN you submit — encrypted and stored temporarily as described below.

We do not collect your name, physical address, payment information, or any other personally identifiable information beyond what is listed above.

12 How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used exclusively to:

  • Encrypt and transmit the SSN to the designated recipient via a time-limited secure link;
  • Deliver the link to the recipient email address via our email delivery provider (Amazon Web Services Simple Email Service);
  • Enforce the one-time view and 5-day expiration constraints.

We do not use your information for marketing, advertising, analytics profiling, or any purpose beyond the core transmission function.

13 Data Retention & Deletion

The submitted SSN is stored in encrypted form only for the duration necessary to enable transmission. Specifically:

  • The SSN is deleted from our servers after it has been viewed twice by the recipient, OR after 5 days from transmission — whichever occurs first.
  • Recipient email addresses and sender initials are not retained after the link expires or is consumed.

We do not maintain any long-term database of submitted SSNs.

14 Third-Party Service Providers

We use Amazon Web Services (AWS) for email delivery and infrastructure. AWS may process transmitted data as a data processor on our behalf, subject to AWS's own data processing terms and security certifications. We do not sell or share your data with any other third parties.
